# StorySync AI — Draft User Study Plan

This is a planning template, not a completed study. Obtain instructor approval before recruiting participants or collecting data.

## Research objective
Compare human-only story creation with human-AI collaborative story creation, focusing on task outcome, perceived creative control, satisfaction, and interaction effort.

## Suggested design
- Participants: approximately 12–20 students, depending on availability and instructor approval.
- Conditions:
  - A: create a short story from a prompt without AI suggestions.
  - B: create a comparable story using StorySync AI.
- Counterbalance condition order when practical.
- Use two comparable prompts to reduce direct copying or practice effects.
- Collect informed consent before participation.

## Measures
- Task completion time.
- Story quality rated with a predefined rubric by independent reviewers where possible.
- Number of AI suggestions accepted, rejected, and edited.
- Brief post-task ratings for satisfaction, perceived control, trust, and effort.
- Short open-ended questions about helpful and unhelpful interactions.

## Ethics and data handling
- Explain the study purpose and voluntary nature of participation.
- Do not collect names unless necessary; use participant codes.
- Avoid collecting sensitive personal information.
- Store data securely and limit access to the project team.
- Report aggregate results and acknowledge sample-size limitations.
- Do not claim that AI improves creativity unless the collected evidence supports that conclusion.
